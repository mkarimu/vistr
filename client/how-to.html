VistrPlay

How to play

Change one letter at a time. Every real word scores.

The basics

  1. On the home screen, pick a word length (4, 5, 6 or 7 letters) and an intensity.
  2. Tap Play solo, then Start. You get a starting word and five minutes.
  3. Roll one letter: tap ▲ or ▼ above and below a letter, or swipe the letter up or down. On a keyboard, ← and → pick a letter, ↑ and ↓ roll it, or just type the letter you want.
  4. Tap Process. A real word scores and becomes your new word. A word that isn't real keeps your old word.
  5. Keep going until the clock runs out. Your score goes into your top 10 and the all-time board.

Only one letter can change per move. Once you roll a letter, the others lock (🔒). To pick a different letter, roll it back or tap Undo.

Scoring

Every real word+1
A word that isn't real (Intermediate and Advanced)−1
A word that isn't real (Learner)0
Rolling in J, Q, X or Z+3 extra
Rolling in K, V, W or Y+1 extra
From the 3rd real word in a row+1 extra each
Hidden jackpot word+10

Each starting word hides 5 jackpot words a few moves away. Find one and the balloons fly. A word can only be used once per round. Repeating one, or changing no letters or two, is refused with no points lost.

Intensity

  • ★ Learner: everyday words, jackpots 1–2 moves away, no penalty for a miss, unlimited hints.
  • ★★ Intermediate: familiar words with some vocabulary, jackpots 2–4 moves away, 3 hints per round.
  • ★★★ Advanced: rarer words, jackpots 3–4 moves away, no hints.

Each word length and intensity has its own leaderboards.

When you're stuck

  • Hint lights up the letter worth rolling, without giving the word away.
  • Next word deals a fresh word with new jackpots. No points are lost, but your streak resets. You get 4 free; the 5th ends the round.
  • If a word has no moves left at all, you get a fresh word automatically.
  • Undo puts the letters back as they were before your move.
  • End round stops the game early. Your score so far is saved.

Playing someone else

  1. Tap Go online. The first time, enter your email once to get your Player ID, a short number like 27. After that the app remembers you.
  2. The home screen lists players who are available. Tap one, or type their Player ID, to send a request. It stays open for 2 minutes.
  3. When they accept, you both tap Start. A coin toss picks who goes first.
  4. You take turns on one shared word. On your turn, change one letter and tap Process. Real word or not, it's then the other player's turn, and their move appears on your screen. Each turn lasts 30 seconds; if time runs out, the turn passes.
  5. The highest score after five minutes wins.

Don't want requests? Tap ONLINE at the top to go offline. Two-player games use the language of the player who sends the request.

Offline and online

The badge at the top always shows your mode. Offline works anywhere, even with no internet: solo games only, with scores kept on your device. Online lets you play others and join the all-time boards. Tap the badge to switch.

Language

Change the language in the ☰ menu: English, Español, Français, Português or Kiswahili. It changes everything: the buttons, the messages and the words you play with. Accents don't count (É is E); Spanish has Ñ as a letter of its own.

Resetting

  • Start over in a game: tap End round, then Play again.
  • Sign out of this device (on the home screen when online): the app forgets your Player ID here. Sign in again with your ID, or your email if you've forgotten it.
  • Lost your Player ID? Enter the same email again and you get your existing ID back.
  • Delete your account and data: send us a message with the topic "Delete my account".
