Privacy & policies

Last updated: 16 October 2026. Vistr is made by Mkarimu Media ("we", "us").

This page explains what information the Vistr app collects, how it is used, and the policies that apply to the app. By using Vistr you agree to this policy and to our Terms & conditions.

Playing offline

Offline games stay on your device. Your offline scores are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

Playing online

To play online we ask for your email address once. We use it to give you a Player ID and to find your ID again if you change device. We store:

  • your email address and Player ID;
  • your online game results (scores, word length, intensity, the words you played, dates);
  • whether you are available to play others, while you are online.

Other players see only your Player ID and scores, never your email. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing. We keep it while your account is active. To have your account and data deleted, send us a message with the topic "Delete my account" from the email you signed up with; we delete it within 30 days.

Reviews and messages

If you write a review, it is shown to everyone with your Player ID, your star rating and your text, never your email. You can change it at any time by writing it again. When you send us a message with the contact form, we keep your email, name (if given), Player ID (if signed in) and message so we can reply, and delete them when no longer needed.

Ads

Vistr is free and is paid for by ads. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in the app's consent form. We ask Google for ads suitable for teenagers. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

The banner at the bottom of the screen may also show ads that we sell ourselves. Those count views and taps per ad, not per person.

Services we use

  • Google Play to deliver the app and updates (Google privacy policy).
  • Google AdMob for ads, as described above.
  • Our game server, which stores the online data listed above.

Security

Online play uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Sign-in uses a random token stored on your device instead of a password. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Vistr is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us an email address, contact us and we will delete it.

App and Google Play policies

  • Vistr follows the Google Play Developer Program Policies and the AdMob program policies.
  • The app asks for no special phone permissions: no contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location. It uses the internet only for online play and ads.
  • Full-screen ads appear only between rounds, never during play, and can be closed.
  • Words in the dictionary come from a standard open-source word list. Offensive words are never dealt as starting words or jackpots, though players may still type them.

Disclaimer

Vistr is a game for entertainment. Scores, points and leaderboard places have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes. Word lists may contain mistakes or miss words; a word being accepted or refused is not a statement about its correct use. The app is provided "as is", as set out in the Terms & conditions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app.
