Vistr word lists: where they come from
======================================

All lists keep words of 4 to 7 letters, lower-cased, accents removed (Spanish keeps ñ).
Rebuild with tools/build_dictionary.py (English) and tools/build_lang.py (others).

Valid words (words_N.txt), from Hunspell spelling dictionaries:
- English (words/):      SCOWL / Hunspell en_US (Kevin Atkinson et al.), BSD-style licence.
- Spanish (words/es/):   RLA-ES Spanish dictionaries, all country variants (GPLv3 / LGPLv3 / MPL 1.1).
- French (words/fr/):    Dicollecte / Grammalecte French dictionary (MPL 2.0).
- Portuguese (words/pt/): VERO pt_BR (LGPLv3 / MPL) and pt_PT (GPL / LGPL / MPL).
- Swahili (words/sw/):   Jambo Swahili spellchecker, Alberto Escudero-Pascual et al. (LGPL).
All from the LibreOffice dictionaries repository: https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries

Familiar and everyday words (common_N.txt, easy_N.txt, start_N.txt), ranked by how often
words appear in:
- Spanish, French, Portuguese: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, built from OpenSubtitles 2018,
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords  (content CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Swahili: Swahili sentiment analysis dataset by Neurotech Africa,
  https://github.com/Neurotech-HQ/swahili-sentiment-analysis-dataset  (MIT).
- English: word counts from freely available documentation text.

Rude words kept out of starting words and jackpots (blocked.txt):
- Spanish, French, Portuguese: List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene and Otherwise Bad Words,
  https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words  (CC BY 4.0).
- English and Swahili: lists in tools/build_dictionary.py and tools/bad_sw.txt.
