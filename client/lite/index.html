Vistr Lite

A pass-and-play word game for one screen. Change one letter, make a real word.

Classroom modeFills the screen with big scrollers and buttons. Best on a touch-screen TV.

Players take turns. A team move can change two letters (one for each teammate); a player changes one. Pressing Reset before the game ends means that side loses, unless the teacher confirms with the PIN.
