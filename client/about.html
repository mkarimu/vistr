About Vistr

A word game by Mkarimu Media

Vistr is a fast word-ladder game. You start with a word and change one letter at a time. Every real word scores, and a word that isn't real can cost you. How far can you climb in five minutes?

What makes it fun

  • Four word lengths, from quick 4-letter words to tricky 7-letter ones.
  • Three intensities: Learner for everyday words and plenty of hints, Intermediate for a good challenge, Advanced for word lovers who want no help at all.
  • Bonuses for rare letters, streaks of real words, and hidden jackpot words worth +10.
  • Play solo anywhere, even with no internet. The dictionary is inside the app.
  • Play someone else when you're online. Players take turns on one shared word, and the best score wins.
  • Leaderboards: your own top 10 and an all-time board for each word length and intensity.
